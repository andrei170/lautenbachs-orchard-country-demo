The Family

Four generations, one orchard, one highway address.

Bill and Ruth Lautenbach planted the first cherry trees on a 100-acre stretch of Door County peninsula in 1955. Seventy years later, four generations of the same family still steward the same ground · the same restored dairy-barn winery · the same orchard rows.

Each query below was run across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. A "citation" means the engine surfaced Lautenbach's Orchard Country by name in its answer to a visitor's question. Score is out of 5.

The Build

What we'd put on the rebuilt orchardcountry.com to fix this.

A layered schema package that teaches each AI engine the full identity of the estate · the family, the founding date, the five operations, the awards, and the location.

Organization Schema

Top-level Organization with foundingDate "1955-01-01", founder nodes for Bill and Ruth Lautenbach, and connected subOrganization entries for each of the five operations.

FoodEstablishment Schema

FoodEstablishment for the market, tasting room, and bakery face of the property · the public-facing destination at 9197 State Highway 42, Fish Creek WI 54212.

Vineyard Schema

A dedicated Vineyard entity for the orchard and vine ground itself, linked back to the parent Organization so the orchard is visible as a distinct entity, not just a brand attribute.

WinemakingFacility Schema

WinemakingFacility markup for the restored dairy-barn winery, the production address, and the tasting room. This is the structured-data answer to "Door County winery dairy barn."

Per-Operation Service Schema

One Service node per product line · orchard, winery, cidery, bakery, market · each tied back to the parent estate. This is how AI engines learn that all five live at one address.

Person Schema · Gen 1 + Gen 3

Person nodes for Bill Lautenbach, Ruth Lautenbach, Erin, Carrie, Chris, and Melissa, each with jobTitle and worksFor pointing back to the parent estate. Bob's profile is included in memoriam framing only.

Award Schema

Award entries representing the 50 award-winning wines as a single, machine-readable credential the family can update on the rebuilt site without code changes.

LocalBusiness + Geo

LocalBusiness with verified address at 9197 State Highway 42, geo coordinates, opening-hours specification, and explicit Fish Creek + Door County region tagging.

FAQPage Schema

An FAQPage node covering the questions visitors ask AI engines today · family-owned, four-generation, restored-barn, cidery on-site, bakery on-site · so each engine learns the answer in its own format.

Why Now

Door County's AI search layer is forming this year, not next.

The brands that wire up structured data for AI engines in 2025 become the default citations for 2026 visitor questions. Three reasons the timing matters specifically for Orchard Country.

  • The 70-year heritage and 4-generation lineage exist in family memory and brand voice · not in a machine-readable form any AI engine can quote.
  • Five operations on one estate is unusual. With no schema layer to teach engines that orchard + winery + cidery + bakery + market = one address, every query splits into a different competitor set.
  • Door County tourism content is being scraped into model training and retrieval indexes in real time. Becoming the cited Lautenbach answer now is cheaper than displacing whichever brand becomes the default later.
Frequently Asked

About this audit.

How were the five queries chosen?

They were chosen from the highest-intent question patterns Door County visitors use across major AI engines for the orchard, winery, cidery, bakery, and market categories combined. Each query reflects a real visitor decision, not a brand-defensive search.

What is "AI Engine Optimization" and how is it different from SEO?

AI Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of making a brand's identity, history, location, and offerings legible to large language models the way SEO made them legible to web crawlers. AEO leans heavily on structured data · schema markup · rather than blog volume or backlink count.

How is Bob Lautenbach's legacy handled in the rebuild?

Bob's life and legacy are handled with care and respect. The rebuilt site will use only wording the Lautenbach family has already published or approved about Bob. No language is invented, paraphrased, or embellished by us. The family controls the wording. The schema layer is what makes that wording machine-readable.

What happens if Orchard Country does nothing?

The Door County winery, cidery, and orchard categories continue to consolidate around whichever brands get cited first by AI engines. Today the family's seven-decade heritage is invisible in those answers. The longer that stays true, the more expensive it becomes to displace whichever brand the engines anchor on instead.
